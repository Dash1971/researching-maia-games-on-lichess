Does playing Maia make people improve? This dataset cannot establish that. Of 2,629 accounts with at least 20 games spanning 180 days, the last observed Maia opponent was a higher-numbered bot for 696, lower for 355, and unchanged for 1,578. First/last opponents are noisy snapshots, not a learning measure or causal test.

05 / Methods & data

Download, inspect, reproduce

The companion dataset contains all 1,425,567 rated game metadata rows in compressed year/bot CSV shards, their SHA-256 manifest, a field guide, aggregate JSON, and the standard-library scripts used to export and rebuild this report. No move lists or PGNs were collected.

Validation checked unique game IDs, the frozen finish-time bounds, complete gap-free date coverage, the filtered-export cap, field presence, and shard hashes. The original private working SQLite database and credentials are not included.

Sources: Lichess profiles, bot crosstables, user-game export. Lichess data retain their own terms; the repository’s MIT license applies to original code and writing. Independent fan research, not a Lichess or Maia publication.
